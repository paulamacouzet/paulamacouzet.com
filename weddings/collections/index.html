Frequently Asked Questions

We recommend booking 4 to 8 months prior to your wedding date. Because I craft each identity suite and website by hand with deliberate creative attention, studio commissions are limited to ensure flawless execution.

Your custom website includes 1 full year of ultra-fast cloud hosting and SSL security. We help you choose, configure, and connect your personalized domain (e.g., emilyandmichael.wedding). After your wedding, the site remains live as an archival memory or can be downloaded anytime.

To eliminate international customs delays and logistical risks for destination weddings, our Collection III delivers production-ready vector files alongside a comprehensive technical dossier (paper types, foil codes, letterpress specifications). Your wedding planner or local destination print shop can execute the physical production flawlessly without international freight delays.

The atelier designs and formats all digital collateral (Save the Dates, invitations, RSVP systems) ready for distribution. To protect your privacy and ensure direct communication, guest list database uploads, email dispatch, and individual RSVP follow-ups are managed directly by you or your wedding planner.

We work with a 50% non-refundable retainer to reserve your dates on our studio calendar upon uploading your bank transfer receipt. The remaining 50% balance is payable upon final design approval prior to launching your live website. Payments are accepted via international wire transfer (Wise / ACH) or Mexican bank transfer (CLABE).
